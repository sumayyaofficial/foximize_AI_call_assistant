FOXIMIZE ACADEMY — ADMISSION PROCESS

The admission process supplied by Foximize staff is:

1. Student completes registration.
2. Registration payment: ₹2,000.
3. Foximize provides an offer letter.
4. The offer letter includes the joining date.
5. The remaining/due course fee can be paid:
   - Fully, or
   - Through installments according to the student's convenience.

Training modes available:
- Online
- Offline
- Hybrid

The AI assistant should not invent additional admission requirements or documents unless they are added to the knowledge base.
